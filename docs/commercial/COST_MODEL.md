# Cost, margin and unit-economics model

No cost values are invented in this repository. This is the planned input model
for evidence-backed commercial decisions.

## Cost categories to measure

Cloud/VPS, storage, database, provider/API and satellite-processing use, IoT
traffic, notifications, support/customer-success time, field technician,
travel, equipment/hardware replacement, connectivity/SIM, laboratory, drone,
specialist/agronomist, payment fees, taxes, sales/CAC, backup/disaster recovery,
observability and security are captured separately when real data exists.

## Contribution margin

```text
customer revenue
  - direct variable cost
  - allocated service cost
  = contribution margin
```

Track SaaS, consulting, hardware and field-service margin separately; do not
average a profitable subscription with a loss-making project. The future
calculator must preserve source, period, allocation method, currency and
assumptions for every value.

## Future validated metrics

ARPA/ARPU, MRR, ARR, gross margin, contribution margin, CAC, payback, churn,
retention, support cost/customer, infrastructure cost/customer and field
cost/customer are `TO_VALIDATE` until accounting, operating and customer data
exists. No dashboard may present synthetic metrics as actual business results.

## Commercial evidence rule

An opportunity must have an observed/verified context leading to a need and an
applicable service — for example, an observed connectivity gap may support a
connectivity assessment. A random sales recommendation is prohibited.
