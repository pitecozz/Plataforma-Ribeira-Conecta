# Pilot Quick Start

This guide is for an authorized Ribeira Conecta pilot tenant. It explains the
current beta without implying that planned modules are available.

1. Sign in through the configured pilot identity provider. Do not share a
   development token or a user account.
2. Open the property portfolio and choose only a property assigned to your
   tenant.
3. In Farm360, inspect the property boundary and the available map layers.
   A map layer is context; open its source and provenance before drawing a
   conclusion.
4. Open **Ativos e contexto**. Select an asset from the list or map to see its
   persisted type, status, source, observation date, classification and
   registered context.
5. Treat `UNKNOWN`, `DADO_INSUFICIENTE`, `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` and
   `INCONCLUSIVE` as useful states. Do not replace them with assumptions.
6. Use the provenance panel to inspect available scene/product evidence. A
   satellite-derived layer is not a field observation, laboratory result,
   diagnosis or flood-loss conclusion.
7. Use **Feedback do piloto** in Farm360 to report an error, confusing
   experience, incorrect data, missing feature, suggestion or useful result.
   The submitted comment is associated with the authenticated user, current
   property, page and feature for safe pilot triage. A feedback submission does
   not itself change a property fact, risk, recommendation or commercial need.

## Current beta boundaries

Farm360 property maps, available scene/product context, evidence/provenance and
confirmed Digital Twin assets, Intelligence Report V0.1 and in-product feedback
are the current pilot surfaces. Rules/risk customer views, suitability,
telemetry and advanced module workflows are not yet available unless their
feature status explicitly changes. A user-drawn analysis area is never a legal
property boundary.

## Safe interpretation

Use the classification and timestamp displayed beside a record. `OBSERVED`,
`MANUAL_CONFIRMED`, `CALCULATED`, `DERIVED`, `PREDICTED` and `UNKNOWN` have
different meanings. Ask a qualified agronomic or operational professional when
a decision needs field verification; the beta does not provide automatic
irrigation, disease diagnosis, radio-coverage or flood-causality claims.
